# Data availability and governance

No individual-level TCGA, PLCO or PAIP data were accessed or analysed to
produce this software release. The repository contains only deterministic
synthetic records created to test program contracts.

- TCGA-COAD and TCGA-READ must be obtained directly from the NCI Genomic Data
  Commons. Investigators must freeze and archive the GDC release identifier and
  UUID-level manifest used for their analysis.
- PLCO data must be requested directly from the NCI Cancer Data Access System
  and are released only after NCI approval and execution of the applicable Data
  Transfer and Use Agreement.
- PAIP images must be obtained directly from the Pathology AI Platform under
  its registration, approval, licence and data-use terms.

These resources remain under the ownership and governance of their
custodians. The ExoPath-GNN authors have neither authority nor a
provenance-preserving reason to redeposit source files in GEO, ArrayExpress,
Zenodo or another public repository. This restriction arises from third-party
custodianship, participant-privacy governance and contractual access controls,
not from an author-imposed embargo.

The MIT licence in this repository applies to software only. It does not alter
dataset licences, consent conditions, data-use agreements or access decisions.
